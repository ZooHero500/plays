Grok Bot templates

A Grok Bot template is a shareable blueprint—identity, skills, routines, plugin settings—not a prompt paste. Recipients still reconnect their own accounts. Here’s how to share, install, and stay safe, with Plays that show templates in the wild.

What a Grok Bot template is

Templates package how the bot thinks and what it schedules. They are not “100 prompts.” Logins never travel with the link.

How to share / install

  1. 01
    Share your bot

    Use in-app Share as template (exact path: follow the app). Strip secrets, private memory, and internal URLs before publish.

  2. 02
    Install someone else’s

    Open the link → review inclusions → connect your plugins → run a safe task before enabling routines.

  3. 03
    Pitfalls

    Long descriptions may truncate (third-party reports). Custom MCP/scripts often do not travel. Re-test after install.

FAQ

What is in a Grok Bot template?

Identity, instructions, skills, routines, and plugin settings—not your logged-in sessions.

How do I share a Grok Bot as a template?

Use Share as template in the app, strip secrets, then send the link. Exact menus: follow the product UI.

Will the recipient get my logins?

No. They reconnect plugins under their account.

Templates vs prompt libraries?

Templates are runnable blueprints. Prompt lists are copy-paste text without skills/routines/plugins.
