What not to automate on day one

Production deploys, unattended cold email, and shared-machine footguns. Keep approvals on.

FAQ

What can Grok Bot do for sales teams?

Research, meeting prep, and outreach drafts. Sending stays human-approved by default.

What can Grok Bot do for engineering teams?

PR/CI watchers, review packs, overnight chores with evidence—not silent prod deploys.

Should a sales bot send email by itself?

No. Default is draft + human approve. Do not enable unattended cold email on day one.
