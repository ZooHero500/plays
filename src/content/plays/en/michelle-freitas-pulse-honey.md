      After each interview, pull the full transcript, find the candidate in the ATS, and draft a short writeup in my voice: the rating, how they came across, what motivates them, and which team they would fit.

      Then stop and ask me:
      - Is the rating right?
      - How did I feel about particular answers?
      - What am I still unsure about?
      - What should the next interviewer pressure-test?

      Revise from my answers. Submit the scorecard only when I say go.
