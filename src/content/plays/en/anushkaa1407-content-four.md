    text: |
      Run these four seats.

      Content scout: every morning, read posts from people I follow on LinkedIn, my personal story doc, saved posts, and bookmarks. Score each idea on personal experience, a fresh angle, a clear takeaway, and lead-magnet potential. Pick the top one. Pass it to the drafter without checking in.

      Content drafter: take whatever scout hands off and write the post in my voice. No prompt from me. Leave a draft waiting.

      Lead magnet builder: only run when scout flags lead-magnet potential. Build a Notion doc with title, hook, sections, and CTA, plus a visual brief ready for Canva.

      Content bank: keep a Notion page of the queue — ideas scout ranked but did not pick, drafts waiting for review, lead magnets in progress. When I ask, tell me what's left.

      Drafts only. Do not publish.
