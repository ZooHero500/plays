steps:
  - "Create one bot. Call it Chief of Staff. Tell it your work, your business, what you are trying to achieve, and what you are struggling with. Work, money, health, learning — whatever actually matters."
  - "Ask which bots you should create to hit those goals. Build the seats it names. A business roster might be research, content, sales, operations. A life roster might be admin, travel, learning, finance. Jobs matter more than names."
  - "Give each specialist one job. You talk to the Chief of Staff. It picks who runs the task and brings the result back. You to CoS to specialist to result. Do not make a Super Mega Life Assistant."
  - "Share the company, the people, the goals, the tools, and how you like things done. Keep writing voice on the content bot, money context on finance, and private CoS notes private."
  - "Log the work in ClickUp or Notion. What is open, who owns it, what is finished, what is blocked, what needs you. Chat history is a bad project board."
  - "Show one workflow once, then let it repeat. Run the morning brief by hand: calendar, important mail, moving projects, meeting prep, decisions that need you. When it is right, turn it into a routine. Check in from your phone if you leave the desk."
