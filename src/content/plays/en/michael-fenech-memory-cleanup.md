steps:
  - "Ask your Chief of Staff how many notes it is holding."
  - "Have it work through existing notes topic by topic and give you a short list of proposed deletes and merges, changing nothing until you say yes."
  - "Move notes where the exact wording matters—standing decisions, rules, veto lists—into a file or Page instead of a loose note."
  - "From then on, save rules, not stories: one clear sentence with the date, and delete the older note in the same step if it is being replaced."
  - "When you say \"remember this\", have it save immediately and confirm in one line."
  - "Keep a Decisions log Page; each week add what you decided, with the date, the topic, and a link to where it happened."
  - "Every Monday, run a tidy-up of proposed deletes and merges, still changing nothing until you approve."
  - "Before it answers about the past, have it check the notes and the Decisions log first, then the chat history, and say so when it is unsure."
