steps:
  - "Name the job. Chuck wanted a 2026 SUV lease: three years, twelve thousand miles, six hundred a month, closed by early September. One bot on a Genesis GV70 Sport Prestige 2.5. One on a Hyundai Santa Fe Hybrid. One on a Lucid Gravity as the wild card. All three trade the same car."
  - "Write the rules before they email anyone. Buyer and seller talk only by email. No lot visit until a VIN has a price on a lease. Do not tell the dealer the max payment. The trade is its own line. Stay inside a hundred miles."
  - "You pull the trade number. Chuck filled the Carvana form himself, screenshot a sixty-five-hundred-dollar offer, and uploaded it. The bots put that number, plus photos of the BMW, in front of every dealer. Ask a hair under Carvana so the store can keep the trade."
  - "Let them work the field. They fill the contact forms, find the human who answers, write back in your voice, and ask for a price sheet instead of come-on-in. Same terms to every store so the quotes land in one table. Nine dealers. Two days."
  - "Hold the line when a store wants the phone, the lot, or a color you ruled out. When one bot double-sends a check-in, write the rule for all three: ask once, then wait. If a salesperson cannot open a photo link, you text the pictures. You stay on the loop."
  - "Share once. When the Carvana quote came in, Chuck handed it to one bot and said share. The other two pulled the number, the photos, and the notes from the same file."
  - "You pick. Chuck passed on Lucid. Genesis ran sixty a month over the Hyundai. He took the Genesis. The bots did not spend the money. They left three best-and-finals in a spreadsheet."
