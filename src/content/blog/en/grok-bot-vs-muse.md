# Grok Bot vs Muse: what each agent actually does

Grok Bot (xAI, running on Cursor's cloud) is a team of named Bots that share one cloud computer and run scheduled or event-triggered routines, while Muse (Meta) is one personal agent with a free tier, $20 and $100 monthly plans, and chat inside WhatsApp ([xAI docs](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot), [Meta newsroom](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-personal-ai-agent/), [Meta help](https://www.meta.com/help/subscriptions/1021145227643680/)). Choose Grok Bot for recurring or multi-agent work you already pay X or Cursor for; choose Muse for personal errands and shopping if it is available where you live.

## Which "Muse" this page means

"Muse" names more than one thing. This page compares Grok Bot with **Muse, Meta's personal AI agent**, announced on September 8, 2026 ([Meta newsroom](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-personal-ai-agent/)). Meta also calls the model behind it Muse Spark ([same post](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-personal-ai-agent/)). It is not any other app or model that happens to be called Muse. Google's autocomplete for "grok bot muse" includes "grok bot meta muse", which supports this reading.

## Where Grok Bot fits

Grok Bot is built around Bots you keep: each one has a name, a job, and memory that carries across sessions ([docs](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot)). Three things follow from that design.

**Recurring work.** A routine tells one Bot when to run a workflow, on a schedule or after an event, and it runs in the cloud while your laptop is closed ([docs](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/skills-routines-and-automations)). Plays in our directory lean on this. Derya Unutmaz's bot [scans X for AI and robotics releases into a Notion database every day](/play/deryatr-x-notion-radar/). DFC369's [Code Watcher](/play/dfc369-code-watcher/) diffs official building-code pages on a schedule.

**Several Bots at once.** Bots can work in parallel and hand work to each other ([docs](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot)). See the [overnight research team](/play/dspaterson-research-overnight/) play: a "Head of Research" bot plus analyst bots.

**Terminal and files.** Each Bot's computer has a terminal and filesystem ([docs](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot)), and Grok Bot can delegate coding work to Cursor Cloud Agents ([teams docs](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/teams-and-enterprises)).

The cost of that design: you need an eligible paid plan after the trial ([Cursor help](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/plans)), and every routine run spends usage ([docs](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/skills-routines-and-automations)).

## Where Muse fits

Meta pitches Muse as a personal agent "built for everyone", usable "out of the box, no technical experience required" ([Meta newsroom](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-personal-ai-agent/)). Meta's examples are personal: sending an email, booking travel, turning a saved recipe reel into a grocery list, planning a dinner party ([same post](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-personal-ai-agent/)).

Two features stand out in Meta's description. Purchases go through Link by Stripe with one-time card numbers, and Meta says Muse is the first AI agent covered by Link's purchase protections. Muse also never sees your passwords or payment methods: credentials sit in secure storage it can use without reading ([same post](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-personal-ai-agent/)).

The limits are about access. Meta's help page says Muse is "in limited testing" and "not available in all locations yet"; you need to be 18 (or the local age of majority) to subscribe and you sign in with a Meta account ([Meta help](https://www.meta.com/help/subscriptions/1021145227643680/)).

## How to choose

- **You want to try an agent without paying:** Muse has a free tier ([Meta help](https://www.meta.com/help/subscriptions/1021145227643680/)); Grok Bot has a one-time usage-credit trial ([Cursor help](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/plans)).
- **You live outside the US:** check Muse availability in your country first; Meta's announcement names the US ([newsroom](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-personal-ai-agent/)).
- **You want jobs that run on a schedule or on a Slack/GitHub event:** Grok Bot documents this in detail ([docs](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/skills-routines-and-automations)).
- **You want several specialised agents:** Grok Bot ([docs](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot)).
- **You mostly shop and book things:** Muse's Link by Stripe checkout is designed for this ([newsroom](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-personal-ai-agent/)).
- **You already pay for X Premium or above, or a paid Cursor plan:** Grok Bot is already included ([Cursor help](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/plans)).

Nothing stops you from using both. Neither vendor says the products conflict.

## FAQ

### Is Muse the same as Meta AI?

No. Meta presents Muse as a separate personal agent with its own app, VM, and subscriptions ([Meta newsroom](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-personal-ai-agent/), [Meta help](https://www.meta.com/help/subscriptions/1021145227643680/)).

### Is Grok Bot free?

There is a trial that is a usage credit with a 7-day window. After that, access comes with an eligible plan; Plus and Free on The X Subscription do not include it ([Cursor help](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/plans)).

### How much does Muse cost?

Free with a usage limit. Power is $20/month and Maximum is $100/month ([Meta help](https://www.meta.com/help/subscriptions/1021145227643680/)).

### Can I use Muse in WhatsApp?

Yes, Meta says you can talk to Muse in the Muse app or directly in WhatsApp ([Meta newsroom](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-personal-ai-agent/)).

### Does Grok Bot keep working when my laptop is closed?

Yes. Work runs on the cloud computer, so closing the app doesn't stop a background turn or a routine ([docs](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot)).

### Where can I see real Grok Bot setups?

Browse the [Plays directory](/) for setups people have shared, each linked to its original post.

### Which is better, Grok Bot or Muse?

Neither is better for every job. Grok Bot documents scheduled and event-triggered routines plus several Bots working in parallel ([docs](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/skills-routines-and-automations)). Muse is built as one personal agent with a free tier and WhatsApp chat ([Meta newsroom](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-personal-ai-agent/)). Pick by the job and by where you live.

### Does Muse have routines like Grok Bot?

We could not verify it. Meta says Muse keeps working after you close the app and comes back when something changes, but we found no documented schedule or trigger feature ([Meta newsroom](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-personal-ai-agent/)).
