# Grok Bot pricing: what you pay, and what is included

Grok Bot does not have a separate subscription. It is included with The X Subscription on **Premium ($30/month), Super ($100/month), and Ultra ($200/month)**. It is not included on Free or Plus ($8/month). Bot use draws from that seat's shared weekly usage pool across Grok, Grok Bot, and Cursor, not from a second Bot-only quota ([x.ai pricing](https://x.ai/pricing), [X Subscription pricing](https://docs.x.ai/x-subscription/pricing), [Grok Bot on The X Subscription](https://docs.x.ai/x-subscription/grok-bot)).

## Individual plans (The X Subscription)

List prices from the official pricing docs, before tax ([X Subscription pricing](https://docs.x.ai/x-subscription/pricing), [x.ai pricing](https://x.ai/pricing)):

| Plan | Monthly | Annual | Included usage vs Plus | Grok Bot included? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | $0 | Limited | No |
| Plus | $8/mo | $80/yr ($7/mo) | Base | No |
| Premium | $30/mo | $300/yr ($25/mo) | 4x | Yes |
| Super | $100/mo | $1,000/yr ($84/mo) | 15x | Yes |
| Ultra | $200/mo | $2,000/yr ($167/mo) | 50x | Yes |

x.ai's pricing page describes the usage multipliers the same way, as 4x, 15x, and 50x compared with Plus, and it recommends Premium for new AI users, Super for daily agent users, and Ultra for power users ([x.ai pricing](https://x.ai/pricing)). It does not publish a dollar amount per Bot run, a number of Bot-hours, or a token quota on that page.

Prices do not include applicable taxes. You can cancel at least 24 hours before renewal ([x.ai pricing](https://x.ai/pricing)).

## What "included usage" actually means

Each paid seat gets a weekly pool for eligible first-party usage, shared across supported Grok and Cursor surfaces, including Grok Bot. The pool refreshes weekly, so one heavy day does not wipe out the whole month. Different activities draw different amounts from the same pool. When the included usage is gone, you can buy top-ups (Additional Usage credits) or wait for the reset. Your remaining usage is shown in the product's usage view ([usage limits](https://docs.x.ai/x-subscription/usage-limits), [x.ai pricing](https://x.ai/pricing), [Grok Bot on The X Subscription](https://docs.x.ai/x-subscription/grok-bot)).

Third-party models in Cursor are not part of the included weekly pool. They are billed separately, from the first use, at API list prices, and you can set spending caps ([X Subscription pricing](https://docs.x.ai/x-subscription/pricing), [usage limits](https://docs.x.ai/x-subscription/usage-limits), [x.ai pricing](https://x.ai/pricing)).

How to check usage, from the official Grok Bot billing page ([Grok Bot on The X Subscription](https://docs.x.ai/x-subscription/grok-bot)):

- Before you switch to The X Subscription: [cursor.com/dashboard](https://cursor.com/dashboard).
- After you switch: [grok.com/dashboard](https://grok.com/dashboard), then open Usage.

Exact remaining credits, reset dates, and how much one routine costs are **unverified** here, because they only appear in a signed-in usage view.

## Teams and Enterprise

Team plans are available on Premium at **$30 per seat**, Super at **$100 per seat**, and Ultra at **$200 per seat**. Each seat includes the same usage as the matching individual tier. Members of one team can mix seat tiers, for example three Premium seats and one Super seat. Team plans include centralized billing and administration, and usage analytics. Plus is not available for teams ([X Subscription pricing](https://docs.x.ai/x-subscription/pricing)).

On self-serve team plans, each paid seat has its own weekly first-party pool; seats do not share those weekly pools. The team shares a prepaid balance for supported Additional Usage. Enterprise limits follow the contract and current admin settings ([usage limits](https://docs.x.ai/x-subscription/usage-limits)).

On Teams, Grok Bot is included and enabled by default for every member (it stays off on Privacy Mode (Legacy) or a legacy request-based plan). On Enterprise, an admin turns it on from the Grok Bot page in the Cursor dashboard and can give access to everyone or to specific groups ([Grok Bot FAQ](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/faq), [teams and enterprises](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/teams-and-enterprises)).

Custom Enterprise terms, volume pricing, SSO, SCIM, and data residency are sold through sales, not as a public list price ([x.ai pricing](https://x.ai/pricing)). We did not find a public Enterprise dollar amount to quote.

## If you are still on a legacy Cursor or SuperGrok plan

Existing Cursor, SuperGrok, and X Premium plans continue unless you change or cancel them. Switching to The X Subscription is optional for those customers. New self-serve customers start on The X Subscription ([X Subscription pricing](https://docs.x.ai/x-subscription/pricing), [Grok Bot on The X Subscription](https://docs.x.ai/x-subscription/grok-bot)).

Cursor's official pricing help maps legacy Cursor plans to the new ones, including legacy Pro at $20/month as comparable to Premium, Pro+ at $60/month as comparable to Super, and Ultra at $200/month as comparable to Ultra ([Cursor help](https://cursor.com/help/account-and-billing/pricing)). Whether a specific legacy seat still includes Grok Bot is **unverified** on this page unless your own dashboard or the current official Grok Bot billing docs say so. If you previously got Grok Bot through a SuperGrok plan connected to Cursor, unifying onto The X Subscription moves that entitlement to the new weekly shared pool ([Grok Bot on The X Subscription](https://docs.x.ai/x-subscription/grok-bot)).

A completed unification is permanent ([Grok Bot on The X Subscription](https://docs.x.ai/x-subscription/grok-bot)). If you do switch, read the official refund and App Store / Google Play notes on that page before you confirm, especially if you pay through Apple.

## FAQ

### Is Grok Bot free?

No. It is not included on the Free or Plus plans. You need Premium or higher on The X Subscription, or another plan that officially includes it ([X Subscription pricing](https://docs.x.ai/x-subscription/pricing), [Grok Bot on The X Subscription](https://docs.x.ai/x-subscription/grok-bot)).

### How much is Grok Bot per month?

There is no separate Grok Bot fee. The host plans list at $30, $100, and $200 per month, or $25, $84, and $167 per month if you pay annually, before tax, as of 2026-11-16 ([X Subscription pricing](https://docs.x.ai/x-subscription/pricing)).

### Does usage reset every month?

The included first-party allowance refreshes weekly, not monthly ([usage limits](https://docs.x.ai/x-subscription/usage-limits), [x.ai pricing](https://x.ai/pricing)).

### Can I buy more usage if I hit the cap?

Yes. Top-ups buy Additional Usage credits for first-party usage beyond the included weekly pool, and for third-party models in Cursor. Auto top-up and spending caps are account settings ([usage limits](https://docs.x.ai/x-subscription/usage-limits)).

### Do team members share one usage pool?

Not the weekly first-party pool. On self-serve team plans, each paid seat has its own weekly pool. The team does share a prepaid balance for supported Additional Usage ([usage limits](https://docs.x.ai/x-subscription/usage-limits)).
